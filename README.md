th# TERRAFORM — Circular Technology & Sustainable Waste Infrastructure

A modern, high-contrast, editorial web platform built for **TERRAFORM**, a circular economy and climate-tech waste management enterprise based in Accra, Ghana.

Designed and engineered strictly according to the reference UI compositions and design tokens:
1. **Luckycharm Reference**: Floating pill navigation, delicate 4-point star sparkles (✦), panoramic landscape hero photography, 4-column metrics bar, and 2×2 asymmetric editorial media grid with year 2026 numeral.
2. **Euphoria Reference**: Centered pill badge, contrasting italic serif typography (`Structured`), segmented capsule tab switcher, and 3-column card grid with elevated solid obsidian black active card and user count badge (`3,800+ businesses managed`).
3. **Marwa Reference**: 4-card asymmetric bento grid with dotted vector telemetry map of Greater Accra, certified material traceability, community avatars with partner badge, and tall vertical editorial portrait card with floating circular diagonal arrow button (`↗`).

---

## Website Structure (3 Main Pages)

- **`01. Home` (`/`)**:
  1. Hero section (Status badge with emerald live dot, Easy Grotesk display headline, Continental narrative, panoramic landscape hero, bottom impact stats dock)
  2. About TERRAFORM (2026 year callout, capability chips, editorial headline, 2×2 photo grid of autonomous rovers, bio-seedlings, pure circular polymers, and Accra leadership)
  3. Mission & Vision (Monochrome dual-column module with diversion targets and telemetry monitoring)
  4. Our Impact (Measured data section across Greater Accra with EPA and Gold Standard verified metrics)
  5. Overview of Services (Segmented pill tab switcher, italic serif accent, and 3-column cards)
  6. Why Choose Us (Bento grid featuring dotted Accra map, certification badge, community partner avatar row, and editorial portrait)
  7. Partnership Call to Action (High-contrast conversion closer with ISO 14001, Gold Standard GHG, and EPA Ghana credentials)

- **`02. Solutions & Services` (`#solutions`)**:
  - Residential waste management (Smart volume sensing & reward portal)
  - Commercial waste management (Zero-waste compliance & custom ESG reporting)
  - Industrial waste collection (Heavy bulk roll-off logistics & weighbridge slips)
  - Recycling and material recovery (Optical infrared sorting & virgin-grade pellets)
  - Waste audits and consulting (Characterization studies & zero-waste roadmaps)
  - Sustainable waste disposal (Refuse-derived fuel & zero water contamination)
  - Corporate sustainability solutions (Scope 3 GHG dashboards & carbon credit accounting)

- **`03. Request Collection & Partnerships` (`#request`)**:
  - Waste collection requests
  - Waste category selection (Polymers, films, organics, cardboard, e-waste, scrap)
  - Collection date and physical address (Greater Accra districts & digital post code)
  - Business quotation requests
  - Partnership applications
  - Consultation & characterization audits

## Design System & Global Styles

| Token | Specification | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **Headings Font** | Easy Grotesk (Standard 400 Regular) | `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Easy Grotesk` standard 400 display styling |
| **Paragraphs Font** | Continental (Book, Regular, Medium) | `Instrument Sans` / `Continental` editorial sans |
| **Serif Accent** | Italic Serif Display | `Instrument Serif` / `Playfair Display` for *"Structured"* |
| **Canvas Background** | Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) & Soft Mist (`#F5F5F7`) | Seamless airy negative space with subtle ✦ markers |
| **Primary Accent** | Solid Obsidian Black (`#0A0A0A` / `#000000`) | Main pill buttons, active cards, floating navbars |
| **Status Accent** | Vivid Emerald Green (`#16A34A` / `#22C55E`) | Single live indicator dot (`• Circular Technology`) |
| **Borders & Dividers** | Subtle Slate Grey (`#E5E7EB` / `rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`) | Hairline card outlines, stats dock dividers |
